# Best practices & quick checklist

Before every cut:
- Verify the correct file is loaded and units are inches.
- Confirm work zero / origin and that it matches your CAM setup.
- Do an air-cut (dry run) to confirm toolpaths and travel limits.
- Clamp the workpiece and use a sacrificial spoilboard when making through-cuts.
- Use a dust collection and wear PPE (glasses, hearing protection).

Quick checklist for Shapeoko (wood, 1/4" bit):
- Bit: 1/4" upcut endmill
- Spindle: 12k–18k RPM
- Feed: 40–80 in/min (softwood) / 30–60 in/min (hardwood)
- DOC per pass: 0.125"–0.25"
- Plunge: 15–25 in/min

Notes on climb vs conventional milling:
- Climb milling gives a cleaner edge on many woods but may pull the workpiece; ensure clamps are tight.
- Conventional milling is safer for less-rigid setups.
